The maker behind it

Every hamper is packed and every charm is shaped by hand.

I started Ruya because I wanted gifting to feel personal again — no generic baskets, no mass-produced trinkets. Each hamper is put together to match the person you're gifting it to, and every clay charm is sculpted, baked, and painted by hand, so no two are ever quite identical.

Based in Gujrat, Punjab, and shipping (or hand-delivering, if you're local!) with a little extra care in every box.
